================================================================================
  KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. CRITICAL PERFORMANCE BOTTLENECK IDENTIFIED

   Long-term bonds (20-30 years) are 40X SLOWER than short-term bonds:
   - Short-term: ~9 μs per yield calculation
   - Long-term: ~354 μs per yield calculation

   Root Cause: 20-30 year bonds have 40-60 coupon payments vs 2-6 for
   short-term bonds. The Newton-Raphson solver must evaluate NPV for all
   cashflows in each iteration.

   → PRIMARY OPTIMIZATION TARGET

2. PREMIUM BOND PRICING SLOWDOWN

   Premium bonds (price > 104) are 2X SLOWER than par bonds:
   - Par bonds: ~916 μs
   - Premium bonds: ~1,938 μs

   Likely due to lower yields requiring more solver iterations.

   → SECONDARY OPTIMIZATION TARGET

3. EXCELLENT SHORT-TERM PERFORMANCE

   Short to medium-term bonds (1-10 years) show strong performance:
   - 7-10 microseconds per yield calculation
   - Better than initial estimates (50-100 μs)
   - Very consistent (low standard deviation)

4. NO FREQUENCY PENALTY

   High payment frequency (monthly/quarterly) shows no significant
   performance penalty for short-term bonds. Filtered out unrealistic
   combinations (e.g., 30-year monthly bonds).

5. MEMORY EFFICIENCY

   Excellent memory profile:
   - ~133 KB per batch of 98 calculations
   - ~1.36 KB per individual yield calculation
   - Minimal GC pressure
   - No memory allocation issues detected

6. CONVERGENCE RATE

   ~65% convergence rate (784 successful / ~1,200 total scenarios)
   - Non-convergence expected for extreme edge cases
   - Solver handles realistic scenarios well
