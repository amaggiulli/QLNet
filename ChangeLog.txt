    Reduce allocations and redundant work in tree lattice pricing (#331)
    
    Performance-only changes to the short-rate tree path. Every numeric
    result is bit-identical to the previous code.
    
    - TermStructureFittingParameter.NumericalImpl.value(): binary search
      instead of a FindIndex scan with a capturing lambda. Exact: while
      times are strictly increasing only the lower bound and its two
      neighbours can satisfy IsEqual, and they are checked in index order;
      otherwise it falls back to a linear scan.
    - Hot-path precondition checks (TimeGrid.index, TreeLattice
      partialRollback, TermStructure.checkRange, TrinomialTree construction)
      use if + QL_FAIL, so no closure is allocated when the check passes.
      Exception types and messages are unchanged.
    - TrinomialTree branchings are presized to their node count.
    - TreeLattice.stepback: drop the dead locals that repeated the
      probability/descendant lookups; summation order is unchanged.
    - DiscretizedCallableFixedRateBond resolves each coupon/callability grid
      time once per lattice instead of calling TimeGrid.index at every step.
    
    Callable-bond OAS workload (Black-Karasinski, 100 steps, one quote =
    OAS + cleanPriceOAS at base/+1bp/-1bp), median of 3 runs, net10.0:
    
      30y, monthly calls  a=0     350 -> 125 ms   188 -> 19 MB
      20y, semi calls     a=0      48 ->  24 ms    34 ->  4 MB
      10y, annual calls   a=0      31 ->  17 ms    23 ->  3 MB
      30y, monthly calls  a=0.03  363 -> 133 ms   196 -> 19 MB
    
    Verified bit-identical (BitConverter.DoubleToInt64Bits) against the
    previous code: 1584 values (OAS, semiannual OAS, clean price at OAS and
    +/-1bp, OASD, clean price at a fixed spread, NPV) over 11 callable,
    putable, non-callable and sinking bonds, two curves, Black-Karasinski
    (a=0, 0.03) and Hull-White, 25/50/100 steps; 492196 fitting-parameter
    lookups on adversarial inputs (subnormals, +/-0, NaN, infinities,
    unsorted and duplicate times) matching FindIndex; and identical
    exception types and messages on failing preconditions. Test suite
    unchanged: 540 passed, 12 skipped.

    Align 30/360 US, 30/365 and Act/365 Canadian with QuantLib (#324)
    
    Three day counters drifted from their QuantLib counterparts:
    
    - Thirty360 (US) applied the last-of-February adjustments after the
      31 -> 30 checks instead of before, so a period starting on the last
      day of February and ending on a 31st gained one day.
    - Thirty365 was missing the ISO 20022 31 -> 30 adjustments, which let a
      one-day interval accrue zero days.
    - Actual365Fixed (Canadian) lacked the frequency != 0 guard, so a
      reference period longer than a year raised DivideByZeroException
      rather than the intended domain error.
    
    Adds table-driven tests for the US and NASD conventions, extends the
    30/365 test with 31st endpoints, and covers the Canadian guard.
